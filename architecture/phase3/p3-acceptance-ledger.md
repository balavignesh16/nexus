## Status discipline

- PLANNED: required scenario/test has not been implemented and verified.
- COVERED: only after the exact automated test and its assertions have been inspected and a successful result is recorded.
- DEFERRED: explicitly approved feature deferral with reason/milestone; not a pass or deletion of the requirement.
- Failed/incomplete evidence must remain visible and cannot be counted complete.

### Source currentness protocol (closes the recovery/inline supersession race; 2026-10-14; uncommitted)
